# Post-fix retest on held-out data

Run on 2026-11-24, after `RESULTS.md` was written. **This is a post-fix retest on held-out
data. It is not part of the preregistered study and it does not replace its verdicts.**
`PREREG.md`, `RESULTS.md`, `langval/hypotheses.py` and `score_texts.mjs` were not edited
(their hashes still match `PREREG.lock`; `tests/test_retest.py` checks this).

What it is for: three engine bugs that the study found were fixed, and this checks on text
that played no part in finding them that the fixes do what they should and break nothing.
The analysis is the preregistered one (same groups, metrics, thresholds, family sizes,
10,000 resamples, seed 20261124), run twice on the same held-back split: once with the engine
as it was for `RESULTS.md` ("before", frozen in `baseline_engine/`) and once with the engine
as it is now ("after").

- English: GoEmotions `dev.tsv`, 5,426 comments, 5,422 analysed.
- Chinese: CPED `valid_split.csv`, 11,137 utterances, 11,127 analysed, converted with OpenCC
  `s2tw` as the plan says, and also scored as written in Simplified characters.
- GoEmotions `test.tsv` and CPED `test_split.csv` were not read and are still held back.

To reproduce: `python3 lab/langval/run_retest.py` (about 40 seconds). Full numbers are in
`results/retest_en_goemotions_dev.json`, `results/retest_zh_cped_valid.json` and
`results/retest_tables.md`.

## 1. What was changed in the engine

No category word was added or removed. `EN_VERSION` and `ZH_VERSION` are now 2026.11-3 and
the engine is 1.1.0.

1. **English wildcards.** A wildcard can now carry exceptions (`EN_WILDCARD_EXCEPTIONS` in
   `lexicon-en.js`). `hug*` no longer matches huge, `yell*` no longer matches yellow, `heal*`
   no longer matches health, healthy or healthcare. All 461 wildcards were run over the
   vocabulary of the GoEmotions training comments and the keys of the bundled VADER lexicon
   and the matches were read. 73 wildcards got exceptions. They are words with another
   meaning (sight under `sigh*`, earnest under `earn*`, original under `origin*`, officer
   under `office*`, opposite under `oppos*`, socialism under `social*`, relies under
   `relie*`, completely under `complete*`), the opposite meaning (fearless, harmless,
   blameless, guiltless, joyless) or another thing (neighborhood, sweater, grandmaster,
   unclear under `uncle*`). The audit used the training split and VADER only, not the dev
   split.
   Left as they are, because the matched word is a form of the stem and keeping or dropping
   it is a word-list choice: `support*` with supporters, `shit*` with shitpost, `fight*`
   with fighters, `excel*` with excellent, `nation*` with national, `master*` with
   masterpiece.
2. **Chinese guard words.** A guard in `ZH_COMMON` that is a degree word plus one character
   (很難, 很高, 很快, 很低, 太難, 太空, 最後, 好感, 真心, 超過, 一下, 一生) now gives way when a
   category word starts right after the degree word and runs past the end of the guard. So
   很難過, 很高興, 很快樂, 很低落, 很難受, 太難過, 太空虛, 最後悔, 好感動, 真心痛, 超過癮,
   很好吃, 一生氣 and 一下班 are counted. The degree words are listed in `ZH_LOOSE_HEADS`
   (很 太 最 好 真 超 一). The guard keeps its cut when a longer word follows it (很難 + 過去,
   最後 + 來不及, 真正 + 在乎).
   All 1,503 guards were checked against all category words: 3,964 pairs of a guard and a
   category word that crosses its end. I first tried letting every guard give way and read
   the result. It fixed some cases (人生氣, 陪同學) and broke others (不得不想 became 不 + 得 +
   不想, 不只是 freed a bare 不 that then negated the next emotion word, 經過世界 counted 過世,
   自然而然 counted 然而). So the rule is limited to guards that begin with a degree word. For
   those, 262 pairs now cut differently and a unit test walks through every one of them.
   Guards that do not begin with a degree word behave exactly as before.
   The verb 幹 ("to do") is guarded: 幹什麼, 幹甚麼, 幹麼, 幹嗎, 幹啥, 幹了, 幹過, 幹完, 幹掉,
   幹得好, 不幹, 想幹, 在幹 and some compound words (幹練, 幹細胞, 主幹) were added to
   `ZH_COMMON`. 幹 alone, or as a swear word in front of other text, still counts as anger.
3. **Simplified Chinese.** Text is now read through a character map before matching
   (`js/langlab/zh-s2t.js`, generated by `lab/langval/tools/build_zh_s2t.py` with OpenCC and
   committed as data: 472 characters). It covers only characters used by list words.
   Another 55 list characters cannot be converted one by one, most of them because the
   Simplified form is also a Traditional character with another meaning (后, 里, 干, 准, 丑).
   They are handled through whole-word spellings, so 以后 is
   read as 以後 and 干净 as 乾淨, while 皇后, 公里 and 干擾 are left alone. Highlights still point
   at the text as typed. The lists themselves are still written for Traditional Chinese and
   Taiwan usage, and the report text and the lexicon note now say so.

- **No verdict changed.** Six rows are still not supported for the reason given in
  `RESULTS.md`: 94% to 99.5% of these short drama lines have no list word in a given category.
  The fixes do not change that, and this corpus still cannot test the Chinese lists.
- 72 of 11,127 utterances have a changed count. 幹 was counted as an anger word 78 times
  before and 31 times after. The 47 that went are 幹什麼, 幹嗎, 幹啥 and the other guarded

  mattered, and this corpus cannot show how much.

- Simplified text is no longer undercounted relative to converted text. With the built-in
  map the hit shares on raw Simplified text reach the level that full OpenCC conversion
  gives. Before the fix they were at about a third to a half of it.
- `grat` on raw Simplified text went from not supported (.50, because 谢谢 was never
  matched) to supported (.92), the same as on converted text. The other rows stay not
  supported, as they do on converted text.
- What still differs from OpenCC, read from the utterances: bare 干, 凶 and 丑 are not
  converted (39 hits), because each is also a Traditional character. After the 幹 fix most
  of those 干 would not count anyway. In the other direction, OpenCC turns 别 into 彆 in
  phrases like 别着急, so on converted text the negation is missed and 著急 is counted. The

- A few forms are counted less often on Simplified text after the fix (哭 6, 笑 4, 瞧不起 3).
  I read these lines. They are 别哭 and 没...瞧不起, where the negation word is now
  recognised, and 笑话, which is now read as the guard 笑話.

## 4. Did any verdict change?

No. All ten English rows were supported before and after. Of the seven Chinese rows,
`grat` was supported before and after and the other six were not supported before and
after. The only verdict that differs is outside the preregistered plan: `grat` on
unconverted Simplified text.

## 5. What this does and does not show

- It shows the three fixes work on text that was not used to find them, and that they do
  not disturb anything else: every changed count in the tested categories traces to a named
  fix.
- It does not make the lists better validated than `RESULTS.md` says. On English the
  misfires were rare in Reddit comments (17 comments in 1,000 changed), so the scores hardly
  move. The value of the fix is that the words shown to a user as evidence are no longer
  wrong ones (huge under "love and closeness").
- It does not test the Chinese guard fix at a useful size. CPED lines are too short to
  contain many 很 + emotion word phrases. The unit tests cover the rule; its effect on
  journal text is unmeasured.
- The dev splits have now been used once. A later change to the lists should be judged on
  the test splits, which are still unread.
- All limits in `RESULTS.md` section 6 and in `README.md` still apply. In particular,
  nothing here is evidence about a person.

## 6. Known and left open

- Guards that do not begin with a degree word still win over a crossing category word, so
  讓人生氣 (人生 + 氣), 還沒有 (還沒 + 有) and 說明天 (說明 + 天) are still cut the old way. Fixing
  these needs word frequencies, not a rule, because the same pattern is right elsewhere
  (經過 + 世界).
- 最後來了 is now cut as 最 + 後來 + 了 and counts a past-focus word. I judged 最後悔 the more
  important reading.
- 幹 as a plain verb outside the guarded phrases (幹這種事, 幹不了) still counts as anger. A
  wider guard would also swallow the swear word in front of ordinary text (幹不想去了), so
  this is left for the owner to decide.
- The character map does not help with mainland wording that the lists do not have.
- `docs/language-analysis/DESIGN.md` section 4 still says Simplified forms are listed per
  entry, and the note in `js/language-view.js` still says Simplified text "is undercounted".
  Both are outside the files this change was allowed to touch. `js/langlab/meta.js` now
  exports `SIMPLIFIED_NOTE` with the corrected wording for the view to use.
- `sw.js` needs `./js/langlab/zh-s2t.js` in its `SHELL` list, or the language mirror will
  not load offline.
